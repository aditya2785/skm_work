A little memory

The Little Candy.

You told me how your mother used to bring you a candy every day when you were little. But you always wanted the big one. You would even cry for it, but somehow, you never got one.
Until your 13th birthday. Your mother surprised you with the big candy you'd wanted. You were so happy to finally have it.
But later, you realised that buying it had taken a part of your mother's savings. And that made you see the gift differently.
I remember listening to you tell me this story. But what stayed with me wasn't just the story itself. It was the way you understood it.
And I think that's when I understood a little more about you.
I liked that side of you. More than I can explain.
That's why I wanted to give you that big candy.
I know it made you sad, and perhaps I didn't understand everything it brought back for you at the time. But I hope you know what I wanted that little gift to say.

You deserve the big candy. Always. ❤️
